# Mountain Oasis: upstairs arrival guide (no check-in steps)

1:41 vertical (1080×1920). This is the upstairs guide from `../mountain_oasis_checkin_v2/` with the self check-in instructions taken out, as Glen asked. It now shows only how to get from the parking to the front door, plus the house tips.

**Removed:**
- The lockbox, the code and the side buttons.
- The remote and the green key.
- The silver key in the door.
- "But first, let's get your keys" and the "grab your keys" step.

**Kept:**
- Parking.
- The lift (yellow and green buttons).
- The stairs walk with the sign pop-ups.
- "Welcome home!"
- All the house tips.
- The voiceover, the music and the same style.

## Running order
1. Sunset title "arrival guide", then a 3-step card: park out front, find your unit, settle in.
2. **Park out front.**
3. **Find your unit:**
   - The two options (the lift or the stairs) over a sunset.
   - The lift panel: yellow button up, green button back down.
   - The stairs walk, slowing at each sign.
4. **Settle in:** the hallway, then:
   - Firewood
   - Wi-Fi book
   - Door pillow
   - Balcony lights and sensor
   - Fireplace buckets
   - Hairdryer and iron
5. **Ending:** "enjoy your stay" over the sunset.
