# Mountain Oasis: downstairs arrival guide (no check-in steps)

1:30 vertical (1080×1920). This is the downstairs guide from `../mountain_oasis_downstairs/` with the self check-in instructions taken out, as Glen asked. It now shows only how to get from the parking to the front door, plus the house tips.

**Removed:**
- The lockbox, the code and the side buttons.
- The remote and the green key.
- The blue key opening the small white gate (that whole clip is cut, and the pause in the voiceover is shortened to match).
- The red key in the sliding door. That shot now starts once the door is already open.
- "But first, let's get your keys" and the "grab your keys" step.

**Kept:**
- Parking.
- The lift (blue and green buttons).
- The stairs walk, with "head left" at the "Where are you heading?" sign.
- The walk past the hot tub along the balcony.
- "Welcome home!"
- All the house tips.
- The voiceover, the music and the same style.
